    Additional Information

    Additional Legal Fees

    Our conveyancing fee is based on a standard transaction and on the information you provide. If your matter requires additional work or falls outside the scope of a standard transaction, additional legal fees may apply. If any information provided is inaccurate, incomplete or changes during the transaction, please let us know as soon as possible, as this may affect our advice, the work required and our fees.

    Where additional work is required, it will be charged at our prevailing hourly rates based on the time reasonably spent by the fee earner dealing with the matter. We will explain why the additional work is necessary, provide an estimate of the likely additional costs wherever reasonably practicable, and seek your agreement before carrying out any chargeable work.

    Fees That May Apply

      Conveyancing

      Our estimate is based on the information available at the time it is prepared. If your transaction becomes significantly more complex than reasonably anticipated, additional fees may be payable.

      Transactions That Do Not Proceed

      If your transaction does not proceed for any reason, we reserve the right to charge for the work carried out up to that point on a fair and proportionate basis. Any payments made to third parties on your behalf, including search fees and identification checks, are non-refundable.

      Disbursements

      Disbursements are costs payable to third parties and are separate from our legal fees. These may include fees payable to HM Land Registry, Local Authorities, landlords, freeholders, management companies, lenders or other organisations, depending on your transaction. Where possible, we will advise you of these costs as soon as they become known.

      VAT

      Unless otherwise stated, all fees and disbursements are subject to VAT at the prevailing rate where applicable.
